# focus

A short one today.  I'm taking this blog in a new direction. Frankly, most of the entries make me ill when I read them over.  They'll stay up forever though.  Maybe I'll look back on them fondly but I doubt it.

I don't want this blog to turn into a pool of unfinished projects.  It's difficult to find the line of saying enough to keep me engaged but not so much such that I lose interest.  I find that when I can articulate exactly what it is I want to do I lose all interest.  

So, now that I am aware of this I will do my best to be better about it.  Only say what needs to be said, talk little about the future, distill the past, focus on the present.
